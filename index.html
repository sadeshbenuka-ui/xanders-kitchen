Welcome to Xander's Kitchen

Experience the rich flavors of traditional Sri Lankan cuisine at Xander's Kitchen. Located in the heart of Colombo, we pride ourselves on serving authentic dishes that bring the warmth and hospitality of Sri Lanka to your table.

Our friendly staff and cozy atmosphere make every visit memorable. Whether you're a local or a foreign tourist, you'll feel right at home with our pocket-friendly prices and delicious food prepared with care.
